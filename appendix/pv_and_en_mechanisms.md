My included mechanisms are all the ABCD Ohio TBI columns that ask if a type of injury was received + an LOC question + a dazed question:
- Hosp/ER (`tbi_1`, `tbi_1b`, `tbi_1c`)
- Vehicle injury (`tbi_2`, `tbi_2b`, `tbi_2c`)
- Fall/hit (`tbi_3`, `tbi_3b`, `tbi_3c`)
- Violent injury (`tbi_4`, `tbi_4b`, `tbi_4c`)
- There were no blast injury youth (`tbi_5*`)
- Other LOC (`tbi_6o`, `tbi_6q`) this category was specifically for other LOC-causing head injuries, and no dazed information was collected about them
- Multiple hits (`tbi_7a`, `tbi_7c1`, `tbi_7c2`) this category had an N of 3 in my sample and wasn't listed in Eman's studies. I assume this is because her sample design didn't end up having any of these three (her sample sizes were much smaller than mine).
